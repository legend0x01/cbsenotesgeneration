Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

NCERT Notes (cbsenotesgeneration.vercel.app) is a free study website that writes detailed notes for NCERT chapters using Google's Gemini AI. This page explains what information the site uses, where it is stored and how you can delete it. In short: we collect as little as possible, we never sell your data and we show no ads.

1. Information we collect

If you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional. If you choose Continue with Google, Google shares the following with us through our sign-in provider, Supabase:

  • your name;
  • your email address;
  • your profile photo;
  • a unique account ID.

We never see or store your Google password.

Notes you generate while signed in

For each chapter you study, we store:

  • the chapter's topic outline;
  • the text of each generated part;
  • how many parts are done;
  • the AI model used;
  • the time it was last updated.

This lets you open the same notes on any device without generating them again.

Notes you choose to share with everyone

When you are signed in, the notes you generate are shared with all students automatically, so nobody has to generate the same chapter twice. You can turn this off in Settings → Share the notes I generate automatically, and share chapters one at a time with the Share with everyone button instead. Shared notes are public: anyone using the site can read them, even without signing in.

  • We store the notes and, privately, which account shared them, so that only you can update or remove them.
  • By default, your first name (never your full name, email or photo) is shown on notes you share. Turn off Settings → Show my first name on notes I share to share as “a student”.
  • If you report shared notes, we store your report and your account ID, privately, so each student can report a chapter only once.

Information kept only in your browser

Some information is stored only in your browser's local storage and is never sent to us:

  • your Gemini API key;
  • a copy of your notes;
  • your reading progress;
  • your preferences (theme, class, text size);
  • your sign-in session.

What we don't collect

We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and we don't build profiles about you.

2. Your Gemini API key

Your key is saved only in your browser. When you generate notes, your browser sends the request directly to Google's Gemini API. The key and your requests never pass through our servers or our database.

Google handles these requests under the Gemini API Terms and Google Privacy Policy. Those terms explain, for example, how Google may use requests made with free-tier keys.

3. How we use your information

We use it only to run the site:

  • to sign you in;
  • to show your name and photo in the menu;
  • to save your notes to your account and load them again.

We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone for advertising or marketing.

4. Services we rely on

  • Supabase: sign-in and the database that stores signed-in students' notes.
  • Google: Google Sign-In, and the Gemini API that writes the notes.
  • Vercel: hosts the website. Like any web host, it keeps standard server logs such as IP addresses.
  • Content delivery networks: jsDelivr, cdnjs, the Tailwind CSS CDN and Google Fonts deliver the page's styles, fonts and libraries, and see your IP address when the page loads.

5. Security

  • All traffic uses HTTPS.
  • The notes database uses row-level security, so each signed-in student can read and change only their own notes.
  • Nobody else using the site can see them.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

Saved notes are kept until you delete them.

  • Delete one chapter: open it and press the reset button (↻). This removes it from both your device and your account.
  • Stop sharing a chapter: open it and press Stop sharing. If another student has since shared a more complete version, theirs replaces yours.
  • Delete your account and all notes: email us at the address below from the Google account you signed in with. We'll delete everything within 30 days.
  • Remove data from this device: sign out, then clear this site's data in your browser settings.
  • Disconnect Google: remove NCERT Notes at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

7. Students and children

NCERT Notes is made for students in Classes 9–12. Signing in is optional: the whole site works without an account. If you are under 18, please use the site with the permission of a parent or guardian.

Google's Gemini API terms require API key holders to be 18 or older, so younger students should ask a parent or teacher to create the key.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
